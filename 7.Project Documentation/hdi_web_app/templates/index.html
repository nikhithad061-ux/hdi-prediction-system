Predict Human Development Index

The Human Development Index (HDI) is a summary measure of average achievement in key dimensions of human development: a long and healthy life, being knowledgeable, and have a decent standard of living.

Long & Healthy Life

Estimated through Life Expectancy at Birth (minimum 20, maximum 85 years).

Education & Knowledge

Aggregated by combining Expected Years of Schooling and Mean Years of Schooling.

Decent Standard of Living

Represented by Gross National Income (GNI) per capita adjusted for PPP purchasing power.
